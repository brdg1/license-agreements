# COMMUNITY DESIGN CONTRIBUTOR AGREEMENT
(Designer – creates graphics, UI elements, banners, logos, emojis)

Version 1.0

## 1. Introduction

This agreement (“Agreement”) governs the terms under which **Designers** contribute creative works (“Works”) to the **Bridge-Community** server and its related project **brdg™**.

By accepting this Agreement, the Designer grants the Community specific usage rights described below.

## 2. Definition of Works

**“Works”** include any graphic or visual content created for the Community, including but not limited to:
- **Emojis**
- **Icons**
- **Banners**
- **Logos**
- **Illustrations**
- **Character art**
- **UI elements**
- **Animations**
- **Any other design deliverables**

## 3. Ownership & Originality

The Designer confirms that:
1. They are the **original creator** of the Works.
2. The Works do not **violate any third-party rights**.
3. They are authorized to grant the Community the rights described in this Agreement.

## 4. License Grant

The Designer grants the Community a **worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive, transferable license** to:
- **Use** the Works
- **Modify** or adapt the Works for technical or branding purposes
- **Display and distribute** the Works
- Use the Works on the **server, websites, promotional material, and social media**
- Incorporate the Works into the **brdg™ brand identity**

This is a **license**, not a transfer of copyright ownership.

## 5. Name Attribution

The Designer may choose whether to:
- **Remain anonymous**
- **Be credited** (e.g., username, alias)

## 6. Irrevocability

Once Works have been submitted and used, the granted rights **cannot be revoked** for previously published content.

## 7. Compensation

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, contributions are **voluntary and unpaid**.

## 8. Agreement Changes

The Community may **update this Agreement**. Designers must **accept the new version** before submitting further Works.

## 9. Acceptance

To accept this Agreement, the Designer must post the following in the designated channel:

**“I accept the Community Design Contributor Agreement, Version 1.0.”**
