# COMMUNITY STAFF & MODERATION AGREEMENT
(Staff – moderators, support members, helpers)

Version 1.0

## 1. Role of Staff

Staff members agree to **maintain order**, **support users**, and **uphold the rules and standards** of the **Bridge-Community** and the **brdg™** project.  
They act as representatives of the Community and are expected to behave **professionally, responsibly, and in the best interest of the server and its members at all times**.

## 2. Responsibilities

Staff members must:
- **Enforce rules fairly and consistently**, applying server policies without favoritism
- **Protect user privacy** and never share private information
- **Act respectfully and professionally** in all interactions
- **Avoid abuse of power**, including exploiting staff permissions or influence
- **Report incidents** promptly to higher staff or the server owner
- **Maintain confidentiality** regarding internal discussions, planning, or unresolved issues
- **Support members** by answering questions, providing guidance, and assisting with conflicts
- **Monitor channels** for compliance with rules, inappropriate behavior, spam, harassment, or scams
- **Assist in moderation decisions**, such as warnings, mutes, or temporary bans, in line with Community guidelines
- **Document important events or decisions** when necessary to maintain transparency and record-keeping

## 3. Behavior and Conduct

Staff members must:
- Lead by **example**, demonstrating the values and standards of the Community
- **Stay calm and impartial** during disputes or conflicts
- **Use private channels responsibly** to discuss moderation matters
- **Follow escalation procedures** when situations exceed their authority
- **Maintain integrity**, avoiding conflicts of interest or personal bias

## 4. Access and Use of Tools

Staff members may have access to administrative tools, moderation logs, private channels, and sensitive information.  
They must not:
- **Misuse permissions or tools** for personal gain
- **Share confidential information** outside authorized channels
- **Alter or delete records** without proper authorization

All access can be **revoked at any time** if misuse is suspected.

## 5. Decision-Making and Escalation

Staff must act **in the best interest of the Community**.  
When uncertain about actions:
- Seek advice from **higher staff or server owner**
- Follow established **moderation guidelines**
- Avoid unilateral decisions that could negatively impact members or server integrity

## 6. Voluntary Position

Staff roles are **unpaid volunteer positions**, unless explicitly agreed otherwise.  
Participation is voluntary, and members may step down at any time with notice.

## 7. Removal and Accountability

The Community may remove staff members **at any time** for reasons including, but not limited to:
- Breach of this Agreement
- Abuse of privileges
- Inactivity or failure to fulfill responsibilities
- Conduct damaging to the Community

Staff members are expected to accept decisions regarding their position **without dispute**, and may appeal or provide feedback through designated channels if necessary.

## 8. Reporting and Communication

Staff are required to:
- Report significant incidents promptly
- Communicate clearly and professionally with peers and members
- Maintain logs or summaries of major moderation actions when requested
- Participate in staff discussions and follow staff meeting guidelines

## 9. Acceptance

To accept this Agreement, staff members must post:

**“I accept the Community Staff & Moderation Agreement, Version 1.0.”**
